# Protocol v1: Public Breast Cancer Cohort-Overlap Resource

**Freeze date:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** Frozen after feasibility and method-development analyses; prospective
application to future GEO updates must not change these rules without a versioned
amendment.

## Scope

The resource is defined by the four GEO DataSets queries in
`config/discovery_queries.json`, each capped at 500 records and retrieved on the
freeze date. The resulting 540 unique GSE records are the release-1 discovery
universe. This is not all of GEO and not a manually adjudicated list of clinical
neoadjuvant cohorts.

The 11 mandatory seed series are a method-development and positive-control set.
They were selected because prior work suggested source/derivative relationships.
They are not an independent validation set.

## Primary resource endpoint

The primary endpoint is the number and identity of GSE pairs with direct reuse of
at least one exact GSM accession within the frozen universe. Exact reuse means the
same deposited GEO sample record occurs in both GSE records. It does not, by
itself, establish whether reuse was appropriately disclosed in a publication.

## Secondary evidence

1. **Exact specific-title candidate:** normalized sample titles are identical,
   contain a namespaced alphanumeric token (rather than generic labels such as
   “patient 10” or “tumor 1”), have at least six characters, are unique within
   every implicated series, and refer to different GSM accessions. Common cell-
   line names are excluded, and terminal positive/negative signs are preserved.
2. **Seed-family identifier candidate:** a sample-specific alias is extracted
   from a title, explicit source identifier, or identifier-labeled
   characteristic. Numeric identifiers are interpreted only with a documented
   study namespace.
3. **Expression corroboration:** for GPL96 seed-family candidates, 2,048 probes
   are selected from the common probe intersection using the fixed SHA-256 seed
   `breast-cohort-leakage-resource-v1`. Within-sample ordinal probe ranks are
   compared by Pearson correlation (equivalent here to a rank correlation).
   Five deterministic nonmatched controls are generated per candidate edge.
   A candidate is confirmed when it is the reciprocal top-1 fingerprint match or
   has correlation at least 0.98 and exceeds the pair-specific null 99th
   percentile by at least 0.02. It is supported when both directional ranks are
   at most 3, or correlation is at least 0.95 and exceeds the null 99th percentile
   by at least 0.01. Otherwise it is not corroborated.

These expression thresholds were engineered on the seed positive-control family
and are therefore not an unbiased estimate of sensitivity or specificity in new
platforms.

## Series-pair release tier

- **High-confidence overlap:** at least one exact GSM reuse or confirmed
  expression-fingerprint link.
- **Probable overlap:** at least one supported expression link and no
  high-confidence evidence.
- **Manual review required:** exact specific-title candidates or identifier-only
  candidates without expression corroboration.
- **No detected evidence:** no edge in this release. This wording must never be
  changed to “independent.”

## Analysis protections

- Treatment response, survival, and model performance are not used for identity
  matching.
- Raw deposited strings are retained beside normalized fields.
- Ambiguous title collisions and within-series duplicate aliases do not form
  identity edges.
- Expression evidence corroborates identifiers; expression similarity alone does
  not initiate an identity link in release 1.
- Exact GSM containment is described separately from partial overlap.
- Counts will not be extrapolated beyond the frozen query universe.

## Planned updates

Future releases will apply the same rules to a dated GEO refresh, add manually
adjudicated provenance from source publications, and validate cross-platform
fingerprints on a held-out set. Any rule change requires a new protocol version
and a complete rerun.
