# Protocol v2 Amendment: Comprehensive Breast Cancer Expression Registry

**Freeze date:** 2026-09-27  
**Relationship to v1:** Protocol v1 and all release-1 outputs are preserved. This
amendment broadens the metadata registry after feasibility showed that a complete
metadata-only retrieval was practical. Seed expression thresholds are unchanged.

## Comprehensive universe

Release 2 uses one NCBI GEO DataSets query:

`breast cancer[All Fields] AND Homo sapiens[Organism] AND gse[Filter] AND
("Expression profiling by array"[DataSet Type] OR "Expression profiling by high
throughput sequencing"[DataSet Type])`

All returned records are included; no outcome, model performance, or observed
overlap is used for inclusion. The freeze returned 5,931 GSE records. This is a
query-defined breast-cancer expression universe, not all of GEO and not a
manually curated clinical-cohort inventory.

## Primary endpoint

The primary endpoint remains direct repository overlap: the number of GSE pairs
sharing at least one exact GSM accession. Pair structure is complete containment
(100% of the smaller series), near containment (80% to <100%), or partial
overlap (<80%). Exact overlap is descriptive and does not imply inappropriate
use or inadequate disclosure.

## Secondary evidence and validation

The v1 conservative title rule, seed-family alias rule, probe selection, negative
controls, and expression thresholds are retained. Title candidates are never
promoted without exact-GSM or confirmed-expression evidence. The 11 seed series
remain a method-development set, not independent validation.

## Release tier

- High confidence: exact GSM overlap or confirmed seed expression link.
- Probable: supported seed expression link without high-confidence evidence.
- Manual review: specific-title or uncorroborated identifier evidence only.
- No detected evidence: pair absent from the lookup; never described as proof of
  independence.

## Change-control rule

Release-2 counts and figures must be regenerated from the saved 5,931-record API
response. Future GEO updates require a new dated release. Rules cannot be changed
in response to attractive or unfavorable pair counts without a versioned
amendment and rerun.

## Post-audit descriptive checks

Following external critique of the draft, we added three descriptive audits
without changing release membership or evidence tiers: (1) recovery of exact-
GSM positive pairs by the frozen title rule, with deterministic different-GSM
background pairs; (2) application of the unchanged expression rules to all
deterministic nonmatches; and (3) enumeration of platform coverage. These are
reported as internal validation and scope diagnostics. They are not a new
independent test set, do not alter the locked thresholds, and cannot support a
manual precision, RNA-seq accuracy, or cross-platform accuracy claim.
