## Editorial decision point

The corrected manuscript supports a narrow reproducibility-resource claim. The
evaluation showed exact baseline reproduction and workflow consolidation but
only one unresolved candidate beyond direct GSM intersection. Submission may be
reasonable as a Resource Report, but acceptance is uncertain and the work
should not be marketed as a validated patient-identity detector.
