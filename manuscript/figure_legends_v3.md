# Figure legends — release 3 manuscript

**Figure 1. Largest direct accession overlaps.** The 20 GSE pairs with the
largest numbers of shared GEO sample accessions (GSMs) in the frozen 5,931-series
universe. Sharing a GSM establishes reuse of the same repository sample record;
it does not by itself establish undisclosed patient duplication.

**Figure 2. Release 3 separates direct overlap from candidate evidence.** The
1,950 lookup rows comprise 1,165 direct GSM-overlap pairs, two documented GEO
reuse relationships without a direct-overlap edge, and 783 relationships
requiring manual review. Expression-only candidates cannot enter a verified
identity class.

**Figure 3. Prespecified cohort-selection evaluation.** A metadata-only rule
selected 30 studies and 435 pairs. Direct GSM intersection and the checker found
the same two overlaps with zero count mismatches; both were GEO-declared
SuperSeries/subseries relationships. The registry added one unresolved title
candidate beyond direct intersection.

**Figure 4. Expression rules provide candidate corroboration, not identity
labels.** Among 385 development identifier candidates, 304 exceeded the stronger
rule, 12 exceeded only the weaker rule, and 69 were not corroborated. Among 1,925
dependent presumed-nonmatch controls, zero exceeded the stronger rule and four
exceeded only the weaker rule. No false-confirmation probability is estimated.
