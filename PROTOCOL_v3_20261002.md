# Release 3 correction and bounded resource-evaluation protocol

Protocol date: 2026-10-02  
Status: frozen before running the bounded evaluation

## Purpose

Release 3 corrects the operational interpretation of expression-fingerprint
results in release 2 and evaluates the practical value of the accession
registry for screening proposed development/validation cohorts. Releases 1 and
2, their reports, and their manifests remain unchanged as provenance records.

## Corrected evidence semantics

1. An exact GSM accession shared by two GSE records is **direct accession
   overlap**. It establishes reuse of the same GEO sample record; it does not by
   itself prove an unreported duplicate patient.
2. An explicit GEO statement that one series reuses or normalizes data from
   another is a **documented GEO reuse relationship**. This is provenance
   evidence and is kept separate from sample identity.
3. A different-accession identifier candidate that passes a prespecified
   expression rule is an **expression-corroborated candidate**. It is not a
   verified same-specimen link and cannot promote a series pair to a confirmed
   or high-confidence identity tier.
4. Title and identifier candidates without direct accession overlap remain
   **manual-review candidates**. Missing evidence is not evidence of
   independence.
5. The development controls are reported descriptively: zero of 1,925 met the
   stronger expression rule and four met the weaker rule. No binomial
   rule-of-three bound or extrapolated false-confirmation count will be used.

## Prespecified cohort-selection evaluation

The source universe is the frozen 5,931-series inventory. Selection uses only
inventory metadata and does not use overlap, title-alias, fingerprint, or
registry results.

Eligible records must:

- contain at least 100 GEO samples;
- have a title containing at least one clinical-cohort term: `patient`,
  `tumor`, `tumour`, `cohort`, `clinical`, `metastatic`, `neoadjuvant`,
  `survival`, `therapy`, or `biomarker`;
- not have a title containing a laboratory-only term: `cell line`, `cell-line`,
  `xenograft`, `organoid`, `single-cell`, `single cell`, `crispr`, `in vitro`,
  `mouse`, `mice`, `perturb`, `kinase inhibitor`, or `platform evaluation`.

Eligible records are separated into array and high-throughput-sequencing
strata using the frozen `data_type` field, sorted by descending sample count
and then accession, and the first 15 from each stratum are retained. If a
stratum contains fewer than 15 eligible records, all eligible records from that
stratum are retained. All unordered pairs among the selected records are the
screening set.

## Comparators and outcomes

For every selected pair, three sources are assessed:

1. a direct intersection of GSM lists reconstructed from the frozen GEO JSON;
2. documented GEO relationships available in public series records, including
   SuperSeries/subseries and explicit reanalysis/reuse statements;
3. the corrected release-3 checker.

Primary outcomes are the number of pairs detected by each source, agreement of
the checker with direct GSM intersection, and the number and type of findings
added beyond direct intersection. Findings are classified as direct accession
overlap, declared SuperSeries/subseries organization, explicit reanalysis or
data reuse, repeated-material candidate, or unresolved identity candidate.

An actionable finding changes the proposed workflow by requiring
de-duplication, selection of only one record, or documented manual review before
calling the cohorts independent. Ordinary declared reuse is not labeled hidden
leakage. If the corrected checker adds only routine intersections or
convenience, that unfavorable result will be reported.

## Change control

The eligibility rule, sample sizes, comparators, and evidence semantics above
will not be changed after evaluation outputs are inspected. Any implementation
clarification will be recorded in the computational report without modifying
these definitions.
